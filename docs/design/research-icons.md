## Generated research illustrations

The two general research articles use literal report/chart illustrations generated with built-in imagegen, stored in `public/research-icons/`. These are editorial illustrations, not screenshots or measured results.

### how-we-score-github-accounts

A small developer evaluation REPORT CARD, front-on and instantly recognizable as a document: rounded rectangular off-white sheet, charcoal outline, a simple profile silhouette at upper left, a compact semicircular score gauge at upper right, exactly six horizontal score bars underneath. Article is about how ghfind scores a GitHub developer from 0 to 100 using six public-contribution dimensions, not about networking. The score bars are muted warm gray, one understated dusty amber highlight. No numeric score because this is a methodology explanation, not an actual person's result. No connected dots, no radial hubs, no gears, no code-bracket symbol. One report card is the entire icon. Flat simple 2D editorial icon, neutral gray with muted ochre accent, crisp large shapes readable at 48px. Transparent background. No 3D, shine, text or tiny decoration.

An empirical research histogram, five vertical bars of different heights on a simple baseline, with ONE magnifying glass inspecting the rightmost small bar. Article is a statistical study measuring suspicious GitHub contribution patterns in 18,947 accounts; the emphasis is measurement of a distribution, not claiming every account is fraudulent. Gray bars with one subtle dusty amber bar. No people, no robot, no network diagram, no shield or verdict checkmark. One clear chart with one clear magnifying glass. Flat simple 2D editorial icon, neutral gray with muted ochre accent, crisp large shapes readable at 48px. Transparent background. No 3D, shine, text or tiny decoration.
